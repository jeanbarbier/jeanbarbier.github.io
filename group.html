Join us

PhD
Apply to the PhD program in Theoretical and Scientific Data Science at SISSA, which opens a call every year, and mention the group in your application. Write to me beforehand if you want to discuss possible projects with the group.
Master's thesis
Contact me directly with a CV and a few lines on what you would like to work on.
Postdoc
Openings are announced on this site and by ICTP. Spontaneous enquiries are welcome.
Diploma
Several group members came through ICTP's one-year pre-PhD Postgraduate Diploma Programme in Quantitative Life Sciences; the diploma in Mathematics is another route into the group. Scholarships are available for students from developing countries.
