I am a Research Scientist (tenured Associate Professor) at the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) in Trieste, in the Quantitative Life Sciences and Mathematics sections, and a member of the Theoretical and Scientific Data Science group at SISSA.

The ICTP is a UNESCO institute whose mission is not only the research excellence, but also teaching and scientific capacity building for the developing world: ICTP really is a special place to do top research and make a positive impact, worldwide.

With my group I study learning in neural networks using the statistical physics of disordered systems, random matrix theory and information theory. We look for sharp answers: how much data a model needs, which phase transitions it goes through as it learns, and how close efficient algorithms come to optimal performance.

Before ICTP I did my PhD at ENS Paris and a postdoc at EPFL.
